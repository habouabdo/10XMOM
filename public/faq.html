FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp, a real person will reply.

How do I book?

Go to Book a pickup, choose your area, your Mom and a pickup time, then tell us what you're sending. You'll get an order code straight away and a confirmation on WhatsApp.

Do I pay when I book?

No. We price your order after your Mom has collected and checked your clothes. You then receive the price and a Whish payment link on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?

Through the secure Whish payment link we send you. Once the payment comes through, we schedule delivery of your clean clothes.

Can I choose who handles my clothes?

Yes, that's what makes us different. When you book, you see the Moms who cover your area and you can pick one. You can also choose "Any available Mom" for the earliest pickup.

What times can you pick up?

We pick up in two-hour windows during the day, up to two weeks ahead. Same-day pickups are possible when a Mom is free.

How long until I get my clothes back?

Most orders are returned within 24 to 48 hours after payment. Large loads, bedding and delicates can take a little longer, and we'll tell you if so.

What if something needs special care?

Add a note when you book (stains, delicate fabrics, allergies, ironing preferences) and your Mom will follow it.

How do I track my order?

Use Track order with your order code and WhatsApp number. You'll see each step, from pickup to delivery.

Can I cancel or change my pickup?

Of course. Message us on WhatsApp with your order code before your pickup time and we'll sort it out.
