How it works

From your door, back to your door

No machines, no ironing board, no waiting around. Here's exactly what happens after you book.

Book online

Enter your area and address. It takes less than two minutes and there's no payment at this step.

Choose your Mom

See the Moms who cover your area and pick the one you want, or let us choose whoever is free.

Pick a pickup time

Choose a day and a two-hour window that suits you. We'll confirm on WhatsApp.

We collect your clothes

Your Mom picks up your bag. Let us know about stains, delicates or special care.

Price & Whish link on WhatsApp

After checking your load we send the exact price and a secure Whish payment link.

Pay and get it back

As soon as you pay, your fresh, folded clothes are delivered back to your door.
